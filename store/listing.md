# Chrome Web Store listing — copy/paste

**Name:** Auto-Summarize for YouTube

**Summary:** Click Ask on a YouTube video to open it in default view and select "Summarize the video" automatically.

**Category:** Productivity

**Description:**

One click from "Ask" to a video summary.

When you click YouTube's "Ask" (Gemini) button, this extension:
- switches the player out of theater mode into default view, if needed, so the Ask panel has room, and
- selects "Summarize the video" for you as soon as the panel opens.

Notes:
- Requires YouTube's Ask feature, which Google makes available only to some accounts, regions and languages.
- Works with the English-language YouTube interface.
- Selecting "Summarize the video" sends that request to Google's Gemini under your account, just as if you clicked it yourself.
- Collects no data. No network requests, analytics or storage.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.
