# Privacy Policy — Auto-Summarize for YouTube

This extension does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any data.

It runs only on `www.youtube.com` pages, and only to click YouTube's own
buttons (the theater/default toggle, "Ask", and "Summarize the video") after
you click "Ask". It makes no network requests of its own and uses no
analytics, cookies, or storage.

Selecting "Summarize the video" sends a request to YouTube/Google's Gemini
feature under your account, exactly as if you had clicked it yourself. That
request is governed by Google's privacy policy.
